Forecast assumptions

Opening retirement balances grow for one year, then contributions are added at year-end. Returns are assumptions, with no inflation adjustment. This simplified model applies one 401(k) employee limit to combined wages and estimates Roth eligibility from gross income. It does not model each worker’s plan, catch-up contributions, debt balances, interest, or withdrawals. Savings goals earmark cash rather than creating additional assets.
